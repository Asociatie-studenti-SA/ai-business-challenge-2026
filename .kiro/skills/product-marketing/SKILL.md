# Playbook: Product Marketing Context (3-Hour Autonomous Mode)

> **MIT Attribution (AI Journal Sec. 7):** Adapted from `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` (`product-marketing` v2.2.3, MIT) for autonomous 5-minute execution from `brief/SSOT.md` (interactive Q&A, Paid Ads, and multi-quarter Changelogs removed for speed).

Creates the shared positioning and objection-handling foundation consumed by `strategist`, `pr-marketer`, and `pitch-director` (`QA_DEFENSE.md`).

## Execution Steps (Autonomous, 3–5 Minutes)
1. Read `brief/RAW_THEME.md`, `brief/data/*`, `brief/SSOT.md`, and `brief/NUMBERS.json`.
2. Write **`deliverables/01-strategy/product_marketing.md`** (in **Romanian**, concise max ~1.5 pages) with these exact 8 sections:
   1. **Poziționare & Categorie:** One-liner (`ce face + pentru cine + rezultat`), product category, commercial model from `NUMBERS.json`.
   2. **ICP & Persona:** Primary segment, **Daily User vs. Financial Buyer (who pays & why)**, 2–3 Jobs to be Done, **Anti-persona** (who this is NOT for — signals commercial maturity to BCR/Alumil jurors).
   3. **Problema & Costul Inacțiunii:** Grounded in official brief figures (time, money, risk — zero invented stats).
   4. **Peisaj Competitiv (3 niveluri):** Direct (same solution/problem), Secondary (different solution/same problem, e.g. Excel), Indirect (status quo/outsourcing) — plus where each falls short.
   5. **Diferențiator Real (Swap-Test Passed):** 1–3 differentiators a competitor cannot copy verbatim.
   6. **Dinamica Schimbării (JTBD Four Forces):** Push, Pull, Habit, Anxiety.
   7. **Top 5 Obiecții & Răspunsuri (Feeds Final Q&A!):** Concrete 2-sentence factual responses to: (1) Price/ROI (BCR), (2) Integration/adoption friction (Alumil/Mainfreight/Tech-Con), (3) Data trust/GDPR (Orange), (4) "Why not just use ChatGPT directly?", (5) Churn/scalability.
   8. **Limbajul Clientului, Voce & Dovezi:** Natural customer phrasing, 3 voice attributes (`Ce înseamnă | Ce NU înseamnă`), banned buzzwords, and **Available Proof vs. `[NEED: validare în pilot]`** (NEVER invent testimonials or statistics).
