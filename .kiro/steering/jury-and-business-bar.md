# Jury Personas & Commercial Quality Bar

Every product decision, number, copy line, and Q&A answer MUST satisfy both the **AI-assisted preselection evaluator** and the **5-person business jury in the finals**.

## 1. Confirmed 2026 Jury & Partner Profiles
- **Orange Romania** (*Ana Ciobanu – Startup Programs Manager; Valentina Giurgiuveanu – AI Strategic Program Manager*): Frictionless Customer Experience (UX), Trusted Data & GDPR hygiene, digital scalability, **mature critical AI usage** (never "AI for the sake of AI").
- **BCR** (*Andreea Petre – Group Director; Luminița Radu – Regional Director*; *InnovX-BCR / Școala de Bani*): **"Who pays, how much, and why?"**, realistic unit economics (CAC, LTV/CAC, gross margin, believable break-even), controlled financial risk, clear monetization.
- **Alumil Romania** (*Marius Ioniță – CEO*), **Mainfreight** (*Emil Ion – Managing Director*), **Tech-Con Industry** (*Special Prize*): Operational pragmatism, concrete cost/time reduction in real companies, grounded 3-step rollout plan (zero vague slogans).
- **ISF-ASF, Advanced Business Software, Măgurele Science Park, PWN Romania, PEFA, Incertrans**: Rigor, clarity, regulatory awareness, applicability in the Romanian/EU market.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Quality Standards (Anti-Slop)
1. **Zero empty buzzwords:** NEVER use generic AI filler (*"revoluționar", "inovator", "deblochează potențialul", "fără cusur", "nu este doar X, ci Y"*). State concrete mechanisms and verifiable numbers.
2. **100% Mathematical & Factual Coherence (Facts Sheet Rule):** Every price, volume, cost, revenue, break-even figure, and **exact UI button label** MUST match `brief/NUMBERS.json` (sourced from `financial_model.xlsx`) and the live app verbatim. Any cross-file mismatch is a `[SEVERITY: MAJOR]` defect.
3. **Commercial Realism:** Never assume 10% market capture in Month 1 or `CAC = 0 RON`. Use conservative, transparent assumptions whose arithmetic every teammate can explain in 20 seconds of Q&A.
4. **10-Second UX Clarity:** A juror opening the live URL must grasp the value in 10 seconds and complete the main testable flow in **under 30 seconds**, with zero login wall and a visible 1-click demo-data button.
5. **Honest AI Engineering:** Never claim an unbuilt feature in Strategy, Pitch, or Q&A. Document real AI errors caught and fixed by the team — every teammate must be able to explain one mistake aloud on stage.
